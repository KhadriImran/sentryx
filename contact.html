Incident response
What happens next
  • 1 — We read the code. Not just the README. Usually same day.
  • 2 — We come back with questions. Scope, trust assumptions, anything the docs leave ambiguous.
  • 3 — You get a written scope and a fixed quote. Plus the review plan for your codebase.
  • 4 — You decide. No pressure and no follow-up sequence.
