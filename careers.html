What we look for
  • Evidence. A finding, a PoC, a write-up. Something we can read.
  • Mechanism over vocabulary. Explaining why a bug works beats naming the class.
  • Calibration. Knowing when you cannot demonstrate something, and saying so.
  • Writing. An unclear finding is an unfixed finding.
  • Comfort saying no. To a client, to a severity, to us.
How we work
  • Named on reports. Your work carries your name from engagement one.
  • Two independent reads. You will never be the only person on a scope.
  • Paid trial. The first review is real work and we pay for it.
  • No overbooking. We turn down engagements we cannot staff properly.
  • Remote, IST-anchored. Async by default.
Applying

Email audits@sentryx.example with links to public work — GitHub, contest profiles, disclosed bounty reports, write-ups. A paragraph on the most interesting bug you have found is worth more than a cover letter.
