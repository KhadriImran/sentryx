How it works

What this tool does, and what it can't.

The model is deliberately simple and fully visible: a base fee covering scoping, reporting and verification, plus a per-line rate, multiplied by complexity, chain and urgency factors, with a contract-count adjustment. The quoted range is that midpoint spread by a fixed band, floored at our entry price.

Timeline is derived from the fee at a working day rate, then stretched by complexity and compressed by urgency — because rushing an audit does not reduce the work, it just costs more to fit it into fewer days.

What it cannot model is the thing that moves real quotes most: the state of your tests and documentation. Good coverage lets researchers spend their time hunting instead of reconstructing intent, and routinely shifts a quote by thirty percent in either direction.
