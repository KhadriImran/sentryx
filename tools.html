Tool 01

Contract Risk Pre-Check

Indicative pre-check — not an audit. This is regex and light structural matching against about twenty known patterns. It cannot follow cross-contract state, reason about your specification, or model economic attacks — which is where most Critical findings actually come from. A clean result means the obvious shapes are absent, not that the code is safe.

Runs entirely in your browser. Press ⌘/Ctrl + Enter to scan.
