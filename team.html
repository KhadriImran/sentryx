Researchers

Two names on every report.

In this industry the researcher is the product. An audit is only as good as the person who read the code, which is why you will never see an anonymous “audit team” on a SENTRYX engagement — you know who is reading, and they sign what they find.

How we staff

Small on purpose.

Two researchers is a constraint we chose. It caps how much work we can take, and it means the people who scoped your engagement are the people who read your code.

01

Both of us read everything

Every standard audit gets two independent reads of the full scope. Not a junior pass with a senior review — two complete, separate readings, reconciled afterwards. Independence is what makes the second read worth anything.

02

No subcontracting without telling you

Where an engagement needs a chain or a technique outside our in-house depth, we bring in a vetted partner and name them before you sign. You always know who is reading your code.

03

We turn down work

If we cannot staff an engagement properly, or if it needs expertise we do not have and cannot source, we say no and point you elsewhere. Overbooked auditors are how reports get written by whoever was free.

Work with us

Talk to the researcher, not a sales team.

Scoping conversations happen with the person who would do the review. There is nobody else here to hand you to.
