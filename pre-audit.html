How the 48 hours work

  1. Send the repo. Use the form or email audits@sentryxaudit.com with the repo, the commit hash and the files in scope. We confirm within one working day that it fits the 500-line limit.
  2. Pay $499. In USDC or USDT, in full and upfront. The wallet address and network are on your invoice. The 48-hour clock starts once payment and repo access are in.
  3. We review. The commit is frozen. Questions go to one contact on your team.
  4. Report in 48 hours. Findings by severity, each with impact, location and a fix. Highs come with a test you can run yourself.
  5. Fix and re-check. Send the fix commit within 7 days and we confirm each finding is resolved.

Fit

What it is, and what it isn't.

$499 buys two days of focused attention on a small codebase. It doesn't replace a full audit of a protocol that will hold serious money, and we won't pretend it does.

Good fit

  • Token, vesting, staking, NFT mint or simple vault contracts
  • A hackathon or grant project heading to mainnet
  • A team that wants a cleaner codebase before paying for a full audit
  • A small change to code that was already audited

Not a fit (ask for a quote)

  • More than 500 nSLOC, or several repos
  • Lending, perps, AMMs, bridges or anything with complex oracle or economic logic
  • Non-EVM chains
  • Code that is still changing every day

Upgrading later? The full $499 counts toward a full audit booked within 30 days.
